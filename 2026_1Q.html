GRAND OPENING
Pre-Opening Acquisition Strategy
Content-Led Acquisition Engine

We are building a scalable acquisition engine designed to generate demand before opening and convert users into founding members from day one. Our approach is content-led and supported by full-funnel performance marketing. Content drives interest and positioning, ads amplify reach, data enables continuous optimization, and retargeting drives conversion.

North star metric: Cost per founding member — sustained by occupancy rate post-opening.

Top of Funnel — Awareness

  • Lifestyle-driven content about padel
  • Educational posts: how to play, benefits
  • Behind-the-scenes construction content
  • Instagram + Meta organic reach
Focus: Brand visibility & positioning Arenna as a lifestyle brand

Mid Funnel — Consideration

  • Club showcase: courts, facilities, community
  • Waitlist capture forms
  • Founding Member announcements
  • Lead nurturing via DMs and email
Focus: Build the waitlist and capture high-intent leads

Bottom Funnel — Conversion

  • Urgency-driven founding member offers
  • Clear value propositions and exclusivity
  • Targeted retargeting campaigns
  • Early booking incentives
Focus: Convert waitlist into paying founding members
💰

Budget Allocation Strategy

Pre-Opening Phase — Shifting toward conversion at launch
Acquisition (Content + Ads) 60–70%
Retargeting 20–30%
Conversion Campaigns 10–15%
Budget shifts toward conversion as opening date approaches. Meta Ads: $30–$80/day initial range.
⚠

Key Risks

Primary Risk
Content Quality: Without strong content, the funnel will not convert effectively. Content production must begin immediately and remain consistent.
Construction Risk
Any delay in ventilation system or inspection approval could push the opening date beyond June 1.
Market Risk
Padel is nascent in Denver. Education-forward content strategy is required to convert curiosity into bookings.
